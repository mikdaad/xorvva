Gulf Oasis Manpower Supply — how the business runs

Six functions, one flow, one manager on top.

Their product is ~180 workers supplied to client companies under agreements. Here's every part of the operation and how the work moves through it.

On top
Manager / Owner — oversight & approvalsSees deployed vs idle workers, margin per client, and approves deployments, salary changes & big invoices.

The operation flow

Work moves left → right; the timesheet feeds both money flows.

🤝
1 · CLIENT MANAGEMENT

Win & keep clients

Bring in client companies (Bauer, Jihua, Darkglobe), own the rates and relationships.
Clients & rates live in files and WhatsApp.
📄
2 · QUOTATION & AGREEMENT

Turn a request into a deal

Quote from the rate card → agreement (rates, OT rules, payment term 30/10 days, duration) → client LPO.
Quotes & rates re-typed by hand every time.
👷
3 · LABOUR MANAGEMENT

Deploy the 180 workers

Live board of who's free vs engaged (which client/site, until when); rotate, mobilize, demobilize.
No live view — idle workers cost salary, earn nothing.
🧾
4 · TIMESHEET & ATTENDANCE — the pivot 🔒

Capture attendance from every client

Every client's format differs (scanned, Excel, grid). Make it, reconcile to the client's sheet, approve, lock.
The monthly chase & manual re-entry — the #1 headache.
↓ this one locked sheet drives BOTH money flows ↓
⬆️
5 · INVOICING
Money in

Bill the client

Rate × timesheet + OT + VAT → invoice, due on the agreement term; chase receivables.
⬇️
6 · PAYROLL
Money out

Pay the workers

From the same sheet → WPS basic (bank file) + Non-WPS (OT · food · − deductions).
🪪
Supporting

HR

Worker documents, visas, labour cards, recruitment — plus the office staff. Feeds ready workers to Labour Management.

📊
Rolls up to the manager

Accounting & Margin

Worker salary = direct cost vs invoice = margin per contract; office salary = overhead. All posts to the ledger.

Your 30-second explanation

“Gulf Oasis is a manpower company — their product is ~180 workers they supply to client companies under agreements. It runs across six functions: Client Management brings the deals, Quotation turns them into agreements with rates and terms, Labour Management deploys workers and tracks free vs engaged, HR handles documents and visas, Timesheet captures attendance from every client — their biggest headache — and that one sheet drives both Invoicing (money in) and Payroll (money out, WPS + Non-WPS). The manager sees margin per contract and approves deployments.”
