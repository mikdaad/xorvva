The monthly cycle

Each step maps a real client pain → how it's handled in Xorva.

01

Customer & agreement Set once

Pain
Every customer has different rates & overtime rules.
Xorva
Bauer agreement holds the rate card: Helper 10h × 10.5 × 26 = 2,730/mo; OT ×1.25 / Sun ×1.50 / Hol ×2.50; Ramadan 6h = 8h. Set once, reused monthly.
02

The order (LPO)

Pain
Client sends a purchase order for N workers for a period.
Xorva
Log LPO 10003088/3 → 4 Helpers, 3 months. Linked to the agreement, so pricing is already known.
03

Deploy the workers

Pain
One worker works some days at Ruwais, some at Kizad, some at another client.
Xorva
Assign workers to a site with dates. A worker can hold several assignments, so split-deployment is tracked.
04

Capture attendance The headache — solved here

Pain
Every client's timesheet looks different (KEZAD = scanned paper, Jihua = weekly grid). You make yours, then correct it to match theirs.
Xorva
One canonical grid. Fill it three ways: type it · import the client's Excel · AI reads the scanned sheet and proposes numbers → your staff approves. Attach the client's timesheet as proof, Xorva flags mismatches, you correct and Lock. Locked = the one source for everything below.
05

Invoice the customer One click

Pain
Building invoices from timesheets by hand every month.
Xorva
From locked attendance → 4 × 2,730 + OT + 5% VAT = 34,398 AED. Posts to the ledger automatically.
06

Pay the workers — WPS + Non-WPS One run

Pain
Basic salary via the WPS bank file; everything else (OT, food, incentive, absences, deductions) as Non-WPS.
Xorva
Same attendance → each worker splits into WPS basic (bank file) and Non-WPS (OT + food + incentive − deductions, with reasons). Posts salary journals.
07

Margin & reports

Pain
Is each worker / contract actually making money?
Xorva
Money-in (invoice) vs money-out (salary) per worker & site = profit per deployment. The monthly report — automatic, not Excel.
